How CineMatch AI Works

Clean Movie Data

Movie details such as genres, directors, writers, ratings, and duration are processed before recommendation.

TF-IDF Vectorization

Text-based movie features are converted into numerical vectors for similarity comparison.

Cosine Similarity

The system compares movie vectors and returns the most similar titles based on content similarity.
